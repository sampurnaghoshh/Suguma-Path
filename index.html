Built for accessibility, first

Every feature is here because someone needed it. Nothing is an afterthought.

01

Dyslexia-friendly fonts

Toggle between OpenDyslexic (weighted bottoms reduce rotation errors) and Lexend (designed to reduce visual stress) at any time.

02

Full spacing control

Adjust letter spacing, word spacing, line height, and font size with live sliders. Persisted across sessions.

03

Word-by-word TTS

Text-to-speech highlights each word as it's spoken. Adjustable rate and pitch. Supports multiple browser voices.

04

Line focus mode

Dims every line except the one you're reading — helps with attention difficulties and tracking on long pages.

05

Bionic reading

Bolds the first half of every word so your eye anchors faster. Reduces the cognitive load of decoding unfamiliar words.

06

Three colour themes

Warm cream (default), dark mode for low light, and a high-contrast mode (yellow-on-black) for visual impairments.

07

Full keyboard navigation

Every interactive element is reachable by Tab. ARIA labels throughout for screen reader compatibility.

08

Mobile-first design

Reads beautifully on any screen size. Large tap targets, readable font sizes, and touch-friendly controls.

09

AI-powered simplification

Turn dense textbooks, articles, syllabus PDFs, or pasted content into language you can actually understand. Choose your reading level.

10

Upload your textbooks

Drop in lecture slides, syllabus PDFs, or research papers. Extract clean text and read with all SugamaPath features.

11

Read in your language

Translate any content into Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, and 17 other languages with one click — after simplification if you want both.

Our mission

Every learner deserves tools that meet them where they are.

SugamaPath ("sugama" means comfortable or easy in Kannada) started from a simple belief: reading and language learning tools built for neurotypical people are not built for everyone. We're changing that — one accessible feature at a time.

We're starting with Kannada. More Indian regional languages are coming.
