The Sugama Principle

"Sugama" means
comfortable path.

Most reading tools are designed for neurotypical readers, then retrofitted with accessibility as an afterthought. SugamaPath starts from the other end. Every default — font, spacing, contrast, pacing — is chosen for the learner who struggles most. Everyone else benefits too.

The Kannada module is rooted in the same philosophy: start with what's useful, not what's traditional. You don't need to memorise conjugation tables before you can ask for directions on MG Road. Learn the words that open doors first.

Built with intention

Every feature earns its place.

No bloat. No paywalls. No dark patterns. Just the tools that actually help.

accessibility_new

Neurodivergent-First Design

Font, spacing, contrast, and layout controls are front and centre — not buried in menus. Profiles save your preferences so you never have to reconfigure.

record_voice_over

Audio Pronunciation

Every Kannada word is spoken aloud with correct tonal cues. The Web Speech API prioritises Kannada voices, falling back gracefully so you always hear the word.

auto_fix_high

AI That Helps, Not Overwhelms

Tap any difficult word while reading and get a plain-language explanation — no pop-up walls, no subscriptions. The AI serves the reader, not the other way around.

quiz

Spaced Flashcard Quizzes

After each Kannada lesson, a short five-question quiz reinforces what you've learned. Three question types keep it varied; scores save automatically to track progress.

contrast

Three Visual Themes

Dark cinematic, light sage, and high-contrast yellow-on-black. Cycle through them in one click. Your choice persists across sessions — no re-selecting every time.

trending_up

Progress That Sticks

Lesson completion, quiz scores, and "known" words are saved to your browser. Pick up exactly where you left off — no account required, no data sent anywhere.
